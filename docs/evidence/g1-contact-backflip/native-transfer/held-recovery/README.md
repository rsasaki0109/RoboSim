bounded joint torques, gravity and sole contacts. No base trajectory, external
root wrench or external physics steps generate the recorded motion.

The 500 µs, 15 s rollout rotates -6.278576 rad, touches down at 1.09 s and
stands through the final second. Final upright cosine is at least 0.999989;
maximum final-second root speed is 0.001789 m/s. All 2000 final-second steps
have positive foot impulse and active ground pairs. The final root height is
0.78060 m. Maximum sole-sphere penetration in that second is 1.288 mm.

and the existing assumed delay. Landing gains are 500/40, early ankle
pitch-rate gain is 1 s, and recovery takes 10 s. Capture feedback is off.
